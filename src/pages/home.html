Our approach

Four programs, one method: ask first, then build.

Most projects in this region fail for a simple reason — nobody asked the people who would live with the result. We spend the first six weeks of every project in the community, drawing maps on paper, arguing about priorities and writing down what people say they will maintain themselves.

See how we work

  1. Step 01 — Map

    Participatory mapping

    Villagers draw their own map: water points, footpaths, the school, the place where the road floods. It becomes the project brief.

  2. Step 02 — Build

    Local labour, local materials

    Masons, pump technicians and community health promoters are hired and paid from the community. Money stays in the ward.

  3. Step 03 — Publish

    Open books, every quarter

    Costed line items, contractor names and completion photos go up on this site. If something fails, we say so.
