Campaign 01 · Homa Bay

A classroom block for Rangwe Primary

Three classrooms at Rangwe were condemned after the 2025 long rains. Two hundred and forty pupils are currently sharing one hall, which means half of them are taught in the afternoon only. The new block is costed at KES 1.8M including desks, a rainwater tank and a girls' sanitation room.

KES 1,240,000

of KES 1,800,000

69% funded · walls completed, roof funded

  • KES 500 buys a desk and a bench for two pupils.
  • KES 2,500 buys a roofing sheet, delivered.
  • KES 25,000 funds a full day of the mason team.

Campaign 02 · Kisumu & Migori

Twelve boreholes before the short rains

Six hundred households walk more than four kilometres for water between July and October, and the walk is done mostly by women and girls. Three sites are drilled and two are equipped. Nine remain, at a verified cost of KES 300,000 each — survey, drilling, pump, apron, fencing, water testing and caretaker training included.

KES 1,530,000

of KES 3,600,000

43% funded · 3 of 12 sites drilled

  • KES 1,000 buys the fencing for one water point.
  • KES 10,000 covers a full water-quality laboratory test.
  • KES 300,000 completes one entire site.

Campaign 03 · Migori

The Mama Mboga seed fund

Ninety-two women trade vegetables between Migori market and the lakeside trading centres. Most are profitable on paper and broke by Tuesday, because stock has to be bought in cash at 5am. This fund matches group savings shilling-for-shilling and puts a working float in each trader's hands — repaid into the group's own account, on the group's own terms.

KES 675,000

of KES 900,000

75% funded · 68 of 92 traders financed

  • KES 5,000 is a working float for one trader for a month.
  • KES 15,000 trains a group treasurer in bookkeeping and mobile money.
  • KES 60,000 equips a whole group with ledgers, cash boxes and scales.

Questions

What donors ask us most

Can I choose which campaign my gift funds?

Yes. Write the campaign name in the Paystack note field, or email us the receipt. Unrestricted gifts are pooled and reported quarterly, which is what keeps a mason paid between campaigns.

How much of my donation reaches a community?

Our 2026 target allocation is 82% direct program costs, 12% operations and 6% fundraising. The first audited accounts will be published in June 2026 and linked from this page.

Can I visit a project site?

Yes, on the first Friday of each month, with two weeks' notice. Visits are arranged through the Kisumu office and always include a community meeting, not just a photo stop.

Is my donation tax deductible?

Kenyan-registered companies and individuals can request an official receipt for tax purposes. International donors should check the rules where they file — we can provide documentation of the transfer on request.
